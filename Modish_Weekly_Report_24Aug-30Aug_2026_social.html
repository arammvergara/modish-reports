Highlights & Recommendations

✓ What Worked

  • Instagram engagement exploded this week: total interactions +250.3% (515 vs 147) and comments up from 4 to 146 — almost entirely driven by the Celine Barstool giveaway launched Monday 24 Aug.
  • TikTok had a standout week across the board: video views +49.7% (4,500 vs 3,007) and new viewer reach up +35.5% (3,400 vs 2,509, +891) — real discovery momentum building.
  • TikTok profile views nearly tripled (+178.9%, 53 vs 19) alongside likes up +80.0% (27 vs 15) — engagement is scaling with the extra reach, not diluting.
  • Instagram saves also grew +37.8% (51 vs 37) and accounts engaged +145.9% — the giveaway content plus regular posts both landed.
  • Facebook's top post by impressions this week was the Ellis garden home tour (299 impressions) — real-home content continues to earn strong unpaid distribution on Facebook.

! Areas to Improve

  • Pinterest fell across the board: impressions −36.4% (57,368 vs 90,129), outbound clicks −40.9% (409 vs 692) and saves −56.3% (28 vs 64) — garden season is well past peak and the End of Summer Sale push wraps 31 Aug. Pivot pinning toward autumn dining content now.
  • Instagram reach (−13.1%) and content views (−15.6%) both fell even as engagement soared — the giveaway is deepening engagement with existing followers but isn't yet extending reach to new accounts.
  • Facebook post engagements fell −24.6% (4,011 vs 5,317) and organic post impressions fell −26.0% (860 vs 1,162) — a real week-on-week softening outside of Instagram's giveaway spike.
  • Facebook page views dropped −32.6% (550 vs 816), the steepest decline across Facebook's core metrics this week.
  • TikTok shares fell −66.7% (1 vs 3) even as views and likes grew — content is being watched and liked but not passed on; worth testing a stronger share-prompt CTA. Confirm September's influencer collaboration is booked given softer reach across most platforms this week.
