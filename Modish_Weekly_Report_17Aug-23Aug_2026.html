Highlights & Recommendations

✓ Wins This Week

  • AI Assistant is emerging as a real acquisition channel: sessions up +43.1% (166 vs 116), users up +46.0%, conversions up +40.3% (348 vs 248) — small in absolute volume but a consistent upward trend worth tracking.
  • Paid Search revenue jumped +140.0% (£5,790 vs £2,413) on essentially flat sessions (−1.5%) — a genuine lift in revenue per session, not just more traffic.
  • Unassigned traffic quality improved sharply even as volume grew: bounce rate fell from 20.9% to 9.5% (−54.6%) alongside a +60.1% rise in sessions.
  • Direct traffic held up better than most channels this week, down only −6.0% in sessions versus double-digit declines elsewhere — a relatively resilient base.

! Areas to Investigate

  • Broad softness this week: total sessions −10.9%, users −14.7%, conversions −8.6% versus 10–16 Aug — most channels declined together rather than one outlier dragging the average down.
  • Paid Social revenue fell −30.9% (£6,257 vs £9,050) while sessions only fell −13.0% — revenue per session dropped noticeably. Check Meta AOV and product mix for 17–23 Aug.
  • Organic Social revenue dropped to £0 this week from £855 last week — zero purchases attributed to organic social. This lines up with the broad engagement decline seen across Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok this week (see native social report).
  • Cross-network declined across the board: sessions −19.3%, revenue −20.8%. Worth checking whether this reflects reduced paid budget pacing or genuine demand softness.
  • Bounce rate rose for both Paid Social (+24.0%) and Organic Social (+74.4%). Still low in absolute terms (under 2%), but the direction is worth watching alongside falling conversion volume.
Next Steps — Prioritised
  1. Investigate the Organic Social revenue drop to £0. Confirm tracking/attribution is intact, then cross-reference which specific posts or platforms drove the £855 in revenue during 10–16 Aug. Check whether this is a genuine falloff tied to this week's lower Instagram, Facebook, and TikTok engagement.
  2. Dig into Paid Social's revenue-per-session decline. Pull Meta Ads Manager for 17–23 Aug and compare AOV and product mix against the prior week — sessions fell 13% but revenue fell 31%, a gap worth explaining.
  3. Understand what drove Paid Search revenue up +140% on essentially flat sessions. If it's a genuine conversion-rate or AOV improvement, look at replicating whatever changed (bid strategy, landing page, promo) in Paid Social.
  4. Keep tracking AI Assistant as a channel. Sessions and conversions are both up over 40% WoW. Volume is still small, but worth a dedicated UTM/tracking check to make sure it's being measured accurately as it grows.
  5. Confirm the influencer collaboration and content plan for early September. Organic reach and revenue are both soft this week — a partnership boost is timely (echoes the recommendation in the native social report).
