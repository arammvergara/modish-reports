Highlights & Recommendations

✓ What Worked

  • Instagram content views up +3.8% WoW (110,438 vs 106,375) — reach into feeds and reels is growing even as raw engagement actions dip.
  • Instagram saves jumped +48.1% (40 vs 27) — a strong signal that recent content is resonating enough for people to want to come back to it.
  • Facebook post engagements up +6.2% (6,633 vs 6,244) and page fans essentially flat-to-up (+0.3%) — steady organic performance without a paid boost.
  • Follower growth is positive across every platform this week: Instagram +0.3% (12,977), Pinterest +0.5% (183), TikTok +0.3% (1,536).
  • Pinterest saves held up relatively well versus the drop in impressions (40 vs 48, −16.7%) — a smaller decline than the −11.8% fall in impressions, suggesting the content that is being seen still has some pull.
  • TikTok video views up +14.6% WoW (~4,700 vs 4,100) — a genuine rebound after last week's −14.6% dip. Comments and shares also grew off a small base (+400% and +100% respectively), a good early signal even at low volume.

! Areas to Improve

  • TikTok profile views (−8.6%) and likes (−7.4%) both dipped slightly even as video views rose — views aren't yet converting into profile visits or likes at the same rate. Worth watching whether this holds next week.
  • Pinterest is down across the board now that the connector is back: impressions −11.8% (97,485 vs 110,556), pin clicks −14.8%, outbound clicks −14.8%. This lines up with garden season tapering — pivot pinning toward bedroom and early-autumn dining content per the seasonal calendar.
  • Instagram engagement is down across the board: accounts engaged −23.1%, total interactions −34.3%, likes −36.3%, and comments −76.7%. Content views held up, so this looks like a format or hook problem rather than a reach problem — review what drove stronger interaction in the 27 Jul–02 Aug week.
  • Facebook organic impressions down −7.9% and post impressions organic down −11.1% — worth checking posting frequency and whether recent posts leaned too promotional.
  • Facebook page visits down −5.6% (898 vs 951) — pair upcoming posts with clearer profile/bio CTAs to drive more page-level exploration.
