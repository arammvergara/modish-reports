Highlights & Recommendations

✓ What Worked

  • Facebook had a strong week across the board: post engagements up +10.7% (6,244 vs 5,642), page visits up +17.8% (951 vs 807), and page fans ticked up slightly (+0.3%).
  • Instagram content views held up relatively well at 106,375 despite the engagement drop — reach and views declined by a smaller margin (−11.5% to −13.8%) than interactions, suggesting distribution is more resilient than engagement right now.
  • Pinterest saves (44) and pin clicks (1,054) remain meaningful volume even with impressions down, showing continued intent from the audience that is seeing content.
  • Instagram followers up +2.8% and Pinterest up +0.6% versus the last confirmed snapshot (19 Jul) — modest but real growth on both platforms over that ~2-week window.

! Areas to Improve

  • Instagram engagement collapsed WoW: total interactions down −87.7% (216 vs 1,749), comments down −93.9%, likes down −84.6%. Reach only fell −11.5%, so this looks like a content/format issue, not a distribution issue — review what was posted this week vs the stronger week prior.
  • Pinterest declined across every metric: impressions −12.9%, pin clicks −16.3%, outbound clicks −12.5%, saves −18.5%. Aligns with garden season tapering — shift toward Jul–Aug bed season content per the seasonal calendar.
  • TikTok pulled back sharply after last week's strong showing: video views down −14.6% (4,100 vs 4,800), profile views down −40.7% (35 vs 59), likes down −44.9% (27 vs 49), and shares down −83.3% (1 vs 6). Given the prior week's numbers were strong, review what changed in posting cadence or format. Figures are still manually entered — the Windsor connector remains disconnected, so reconnecting it is still the priority.
  • Facebook organic impressions (page and post level) both declined (−20.5% and −30.8%) even as engagement and page visits rose — reach may be narrowing to a smaller, more engaged base. Worth watching if this continues.
