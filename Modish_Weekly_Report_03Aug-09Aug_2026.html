Highlights & Recommendations

✓ Wins This Week

  • Total social sessions up +16.0% WoW (13,734 vs 11,843) — the strongest weekly session growth in recent weeks.
  • Paid Social revenue up +14.4% (£7,758 vs £6,784), outpacing the +8.5% rise in sessions — signs of improving efficiency, not just volume.
  • Organic Social bounce rate improved sharply: 0.50% vs 1.39% (−64.0%) — organic visitors are far more engaged with the site once they land.
  • Organic Social users up +11.3% (543 vs 488) and sessions up +4.5% — steady organic growth alongside the paid gains.
  • Total users across all social grew +11.3% (10,292 vs 9,246), reinforcing that this week's growth was broad-based, not one channel.

! Areas to Investigate

  • Unassigned channel sessions spiked from 182 to 1,481 (+714%) with bounce rate jumping to 88.25% (vs 12.64% prev week) — this looks like a tagging or UTM gap rather than genuine traffic growth and is worth auditing.
  • Paid Social bounce rate rose to 0.99% from 0.78% (+26.9%) even as revenue and sessions grew — still a low absolute rate, but worth checking whether a specific placement or audience is driving it.
  • Organic Social revenue remained at £0 for a second straight week. With sessions and users both growing, the lack of attributed purchases suggests either a genuine conversion gap or a tracking issue on organic social links.
  • Session duration is not tracked: the Windsor GA4 connector does not return avg_session_duration at channel group level — a gap worth monitoring through the GA4 dashboard directly.
Next Steps — Prioritised
  1. Audit the Unassigned channel spike. Sessions jumped over 7x with an 88% bounce rate — check for a recent tracking, tagging, or consent-mode change that could be misattributing traffic that should be landing in a known channel.
  2. Investigate Organic Social's continued £0 revenue. Confirm UTM tagging is present on all Instagram, Facebook, and Pinterest bio links and post CTAs, and cross-check GA4 e-commerce tracking against Shopify order data for the week.
  3. Spot-check Paid Social bounce rate. Pull Meta Ads Manager for 03–09 Aug and compare placements/audiences against the prior week to see what's driving the 0.99% rate, even though it remains a healthy absolute number.
  4. Confirm August influencer activation. Monthly cadence requires at least 1 per month. Beds season (Jul–Aug) is the seasonal focus — ensure a bedroom-styling partnership is booked or already live, with UTM-tagged links in place.
