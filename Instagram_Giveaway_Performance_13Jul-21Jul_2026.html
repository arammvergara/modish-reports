Analysis

What the numbers suggest

  • The giveaway's live likes (~1,247) account for the large majority of the account's total 9-day likes (1,534) — this post is almost certainly the main engagement driver for the window.
  • The "comment and tag 2 friends" mechanic is a strong structural match for the account's elevated comment volume (1,475) over the same period — most visible replies on the post are exactly this pattern.
  • Story shares (128) as an extra-entry incentive likely contributed meaningfully to the period's reach (36,383) beyond the account's typical organic distribution.
  • The post's comment count (~1.6K) runs ahead of the account-level windowed total (1,475) because the post metric is a live, uncapped cumulative count — some of that activity happened after the giveaway closed on 21 Jul, outside this report's window.
  • The account gained +888 followers (+7.4%) across the full giveaway window (12→21 Jul) — a genuine, sizeable spike directly tied to the "follow to enter" entry requirement.
  • Growth stopped almost entirely once the giveaway closed: the account sat at 12,939 on 2 Aug, effectively unchanged (−2) from the 12,941 recorded at close on 21 Jul, despite 12 days elapsing. This strongly suggests unfollows from giveaway entrants offset any organic growth in that period.
  • Recommendation: this giveaway was highly effective at generating a short-term follower spike and heavy engagement, but it did not produce lasting net follower growth — the entry mechanic likely attracted prize-motivated entrants rather than genuinely interested customers. Future giveaways may benefit from a lighter follow-gate (e.g. weighting entries toward genuine engagement or purchase intent) if durable audience growth is the goal, rather than raw follower count during the promotion.
