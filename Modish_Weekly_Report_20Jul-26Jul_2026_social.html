Highlights & Recommendations

✓ What Worked

  • Facebook had its strongest week in over a month: post engagements up +27.4% (5,642 vs 4,428), organic impressions up +66.6% (2,879 vs 1,728), and page visits up +42.6% (807 vs 566).
  • Pinterest rebounded across every metric — impressions +17.6%, pin clicks +21.2%, outbound clicks +19.5%, saves +35.0% — reversing the prior week's decline.
  • Instagram content views rose +5.1% (123,405 vs 117,452) even as engagement normalised down from the prior week's anomalous spike.
  • Instagram engagement (633 engaged, 1,749 interactions) landed roughly midway between the 6–12 Jul baseline and the 13–19 Jul spike — a healthier, more sustainable level than either extreme.
  • TikTok had a strong week on manually-supplied figures: video views up +48.5% (4,800 vs 3,232), profile views up +90.3% (59 vs 31), likes up +53.1% (49 vs 32), and shares up sharply (6 vs 1).

! Areas to Improve

  • TikTok's connector remains disconnected for both this week and the current week — these figures were entered manually. Reconnecting it is still the top priority so future reports don't need a manual workaround.
  • Instagram reach dipped −6.3% (27,526 vs 29,363) even as content views rose — worth checking whether posts are reaching fewer unique accounts despite more total plays.
  • Follower counts for Instagram and Pinterest this week could not be captured because this report was compiled after the fact on 3 Aug — going forward, weekly snapshots (now current as of 2 Aug) will keep this gap from recurring.
  • Given Facebook and Pinterest's strong rebound this week, it's worth identifying which specific posts or pins drove it and replicating the format going into the beds season push.
