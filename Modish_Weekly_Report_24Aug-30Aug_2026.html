Highlights & Recommendations

✓ Wins This Week

  • Organic Search was the standout channel: sessions up +19.0% (507 vs 426) and revenue up +51.6% (£3,205.25 vs £2,114.25) — the only major channel growing this week.
  • Paid Social and Organic Social bounce rates remain excellent at 1.78% and 1.54% respectively — well below the Unassigned channel's 80.97%, so the traffic quality from social stayed high even as volume fell.
  • Referral sessions grew +35.9% (72 vs 53) — a small but genuine gain worth watching.
  • Direct remained the top revenue channel this week at £4,974.25, holding up better than most other channels.
  • Total conversions still cleared 18,000+ despite the broad pullback, showing underlying purchase intent held up better than raw session declines would suggest.

! Areas to Investigate

  • Paid Social revenue collapsed −77.8% (£1,390 vs £6,256.50) despite sessions falling a much smaller −24.5% — revenue per session fell sharply. Check Meta budget pacing, AOV and attribution windows for 24–30 Aug.
  • Cross-network revenue fell even further, −83.7% (£996.25 vs £6,128.50) — the steepest revenue decline of any channel this week.
  • Organic Social sessions fell −26.9% and conversions −21.4%, even though this was giveaway week on Instagram (127 comments). The comment-to-enter mechanic is driving on-platform engagement but not translating into GA4-tracked site sessions.
  • Paid Search revenue down −21.4% (£4,551.25 vs £5,790.25) alongside a −19.9% drop in sessions.
  • Total sessions, users and conversions all declined this week (−9.6%, −11.1%, −16.7% respectively) — a broad pullback across nearly every channel, not isolated to one source.
Next Steps — Prioritised
  1. Investigate the Paid Social and Cross-network revenue collapse. Both channels are down over 75% in revenue this week despite far smaller session declines. Flag to the paid social agency, check Meta budget pacing, AOV and attribution windows for 24–30 Aug before the next spend cycle.
  2. Add a website CTA to future Instagram giveaways. This week's comment-to-enter giveaway drove 127 comments but Organic Social sessions still fell −26.9%. Route giveaway entrants through the site link-in-bio next time to capture the traffic on-site.
  3. Lean into Organic Search. It was the only channel growing this week (sessions +19.0%, revenue +51.6%). Identify which pages or keywords are pulling this and reinforce with supporting content.
  4. Begin the transition to dining-season content now. Sep–Nov is Modish's peak dining period, and this week's across-the-board dip is a good moment to start seeding autumn dining content on-site and across channels.
  5. Confirm September's influencer collaboration is booked. Monthly cadence requires at least one partnership, and softer organic numbers this week make a partnership boost timely heading into the new season.
