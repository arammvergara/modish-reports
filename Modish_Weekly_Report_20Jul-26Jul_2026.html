Highlights & Recommendations

✓ Wins This Week

  • Paid Social had a strong week: sessions up +7.2% (6,378 vs 5,950), users up +13.2%, and conversions up +9.4% (9,945 vs 9,094).
  • Organic Social revenue appeared for the first time in weeks: £1,121 vs £0 — a meaningful signal that organic social is starting to convert directly.
  • Organic Social sessions and conversions both grew (+14.7% and +10.4% respectively), alongside a +10.0% rise in users.
  • Paid Social bounce rate improved (0.55% vs 0.59%, −6.8%) even as volume grew — traffic quality held up despite the higher session count.
  • Total conversions and users both up +2.8% WoW, holding steady even as total sessions were roughly flat.

! Areas to Investigate

  • Paid Social revenue dropped sharply despite session growth: £3,489 vs £8,532 (−59.1%). Sessions and conversions rose, but revenue fell — check AOV and product mix on Meta for this week specifically.
  • Organic Social bounce rate worsened: 1.03% vs 0.78% (+32.1%) even as sessions grew — the extra volume may be lower-intent traffic.
  • Cross-network sessions fell −20.0% (2,392 vs 2,990) — worth checking whether this reflects a genuine platform decline or an attribution shift toward single-channel tracking.
  • Total sessions were essentially flat (−0.2%) despite Paid and Organic Social both growing — the Cross-network decline appears to be offsetting those gains overall.
  • Unassigned traffic still carries a very high bounce rate (15.59% on 186 sessions) — a recurring tagging gap worth resolving.
Next Steps — Prioritised
  1. Investigate the Paid Social revenue drop. Pull Meta Ads Manager for 20–26 Jul and compare AOV and product mix against the prior week — sessions and conversions rose while revenue fell −59.1%, which points to a mix or pricing shift rather than a traffic problem.
  2. Audit Organic Social bounce rate. Bounce rate rose +32.1% even as sessions and conversions both grew — review which posts drove the extra traffic and whether it matched the usual audience intent.
  3. Check the Cross-network decline. Sessions fell −20.0% WoW — confirm whether this is a real platform-level drop or a shift in how GA4 is attributing multi-channel journeys.
  4. Fix Unassigned traffic tagging. 186 sessions with a 15.59% bounce rate is still unusually high — check for missing UTM parameters.
  5. Build on the Organic Social revenue signal. This is the first week with measurable organic social revenue (£1,121) — identify which content or product drove it and look to replicate the approach.
