Highlights & Recommendations

✓ What Worked

  • Instagram engagement surged this week — total interactions up +64.1% (233 vs 142) and accounts engaged up +29.1% (142 vs 110), the strongest engagement week in recent memory.
  • Instagram comments nearly doubled, +85.7% (13 vs 7) — a strong signal content is prompting genuine conversation, not just passive scrolling.
  • Instagram reach and content views both grew in step — reach up +5.2% to 25,482, content views up +4.4% to 115,274 — audience is expanding alongside the engagement lift.
  • Facebook organic impressions climbed +17.1% (2,471 vs 2,110) and page visits held a steady upward trend (+3.9%) — organic reach on Facebook is trending the right way.
  • TikTok shares more than doubled, +150% (5 vs 2), and profile views grew +6.3% (34 vs 32) — small numbers, but the direction is right.
  • Follower growth held positive or flat across every tracked platform — Instagram +0.4% (13,033), TikTok +0.1% (1,538), Pinterest steady at 183.

! Areas to Improve

  • Pinterest impressions fell −15.2% (95,184 vs 112,225) with pin clicks down −10.8% and outbound clicks down −8.4% — consistent with garden season tapering. Time to pivot boards toward bedroom and dining content for the seasonal shift.
  • Pinterest saves dropped −17.4% (38 vs 46) — fewer people are bookmarking pins for later. Worth testing fresh creative or seasonal pin titles.
  • Facebook post impressions (organic) fell sharply, −57.7% (516 vs 1,220), even as page-level organic impressions rose — individual posts underperformed this week. Review format and posting times.
  • TikTok engagement cooled — likes down −44.0% (14 vs 25) and comments fell to zero (vs 5), while video views dipped −14.9% (~4,000 vs ~4,700). Worth testing a stronger hook in the first three seconds.
  • Instagram likes grew more slowly than other engagement types (+20.7% vs +64.1% total interactions) — saves and comments are outpacing likes, suggesting deeper engagement than surface-level.
